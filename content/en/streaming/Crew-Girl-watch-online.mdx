<Section icon={BookOpen} title="Rowing Terms That Make Crew Girl Easier to Follow">

Rowing is more than the setting of Crew Girl. It provides the show’s main structure for teamwork, pressure, leadership, and personal growth. Understanding a few basic terms helps explain why training sessions, erg scores, boat positions, and timing matter to the characters.

Teagan’s background as a single sculler also provides useful context. Single sculling emphasizes individual control, while a crew boat requires athletes to coordinate their movement and power with teammates.

| Term | Simple Meaning | Story Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Crew | A rowing team or the athletes competing together | Represents trust, timing, and shared responsibility |
| Single sculler | An athlete racing alone in a single sculling boat | Helps distinguish individual skill from team rowing |
| Coxswain | The crew member who directs and communicates with rowers | Highlights leadership, strategy, and communication |
| Erg | An indoor rowing machine used for training | Provides measurable performance comparisons |
| PR erg score | A personal record on an ergometer | Can influence confidence and team expectations |
| Stroke and rhythm | The repeated movement pattern shared by rowers | Shows how individual effort becomes coordinated speed |
