New viewers do not need prior knowledge of rowing to follow the drama. A few terms make the competition scenes easier to understand:

- **Crew** refers to the rowing team and, in context, the athletes competing together.
- **Coxswain** describes the team member who directs the boat, gives commands, and helps maintain race focus.
- **Erg** is an indoor rowing machine used for training and performance testing.
- **PR erg score** means a personal record achieved on an ergometer.
- **Stroke and rhythm** describe the repeated movement sequence that allows rowers to apply power together.
