<Section icon={Waves} title="Rowing Terms Every Crew Girl Viewer Should Know">

The series introduces rowing concepts through training, competition, and team conversations. Viewers do not need prior knowledge of the sport, but a few basic terms clarify why certain scenes matter.

A crew succeeds through coordination rather than isolated effort. Each athlete contributes power, but the boat also depends on timing, balance, communication, and the ability to respond to the coxswain’s direction.

| Term | Meaning | Why It Matters in the Series |
|---|---|---|
| Crew | A rowing team and the athletes competing together | Represents the shared goals and interpersonal pressure surrounding Teagan |
| Single scull | A boat raced by one rower | Reflects Teagan’s individual athletic background |
| Rowing position | A seat or assigned place within the boat | Helps define each athlete’s responsibilities and status |
| Coxswain | The person who directs the crew and calls commands | Connects leadership, race strategy, and communication |
| Erg | An indoor rowing machine used for training | Creates measurable individual competition outside the boat |
| PR erg score | A personal record on an ergometer | Provides a benchmark for fitness, progress, and confidence |
| Stroke and rhythm | The repeated movement cycle performed together | Shows how individual effort becomes coordinated team performance |
