<Section icon={Waves} title="Crew Girl Rowing Terms Explained">

Rowing gives Crew Girl a practical framework for discussing teamwork. New viewers do not need technical sports knowledge to follow the drama, but several terms make the competition scenes easier to understand.

The show’s rowing material includes crew structure, boat positions, the coxswain’s leadership role, erg training, personal records, and synchronized stroke rhythm. These ideas also work as story metaphors: individual talent matters, but timing and communication determine whether the group moves effectively.

| Term | Simple Meaning | Why It Matters in the Series |
|---|---|---|
| Crew | A rowing team or the athletes competing together | Represents shared responsibility and group identity |
| Rowing position | A specific seat or role in the boat | Helps explain different responsibilities within one team |
| Coxswain | The crew member who directs and communicates with rowers | Highlights leadership, timing, and race strategy |
| Erg | An indoor rowing machine used for training | Creates measurable tests of fitness and performance |
| PR erg score | A personal record achieved on an ergometer | Shows progress, pressure, and individual comparison |
| Stroke rhythm | The repeated movement sequence performed together | Turns separate efforts into coordinated boat speed |
