<Section icon={Map} title="Crew Girl review: Story and First Impression">

Crew Girl is a Canadian teen sports drama from Netflix built around Teagan Tao, a young sculler whose life changes after a family scandal. Her move into a new environment leads her to an all-boys prep-school rowing team, where athletic competition becomes connected to friendship, romance, reputation, and family pressure.

The series has a clear coming-of-age structure. Teagan is not simply learning how to compete in a new sport setting; she is also trying to establish who she is after circumstances outside her control reshape her daily life. That combination gives the rowing storyline a personal purpose rather than treating races as isolated set pieces.
