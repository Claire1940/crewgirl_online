<Section icon={Trophy} title="Rowing Terms That Shape the Plot">

Rowing gives Crew Girl a clear visual and thematic identity. Viewers do not need extensive sports knowledge to follow the drama, but several terms explain why training, teamwork, and performance measurements matter to the characters.

Teagan’s background as a single sculler contrasts with the teamwork required in a crew boat. In a larger boat, every rower must coordinate the stroke sequence, maintain rhythm, and respond to shared commands. This creates natural story pressure: an individual can be talented while still needing to adapt to the group.

| Rowing Term | Plain-English Meaning | Story Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Crew | A rowing team or the athletes competing together in one boat | Represents cooperation, trust, and shared responsibility |
| Single sculler | A rower competing alone in a single-person boat | Highlights Teagan’s individual skill and independence |
| Coxswain | Team member who directs the boat and communicates commands | Connects leadership, strategy, and communication |
| Erg | Indoor rowing machine used for training and testing | Creates measurable competition away from the water |
| PR erg score | A rower’s personal record on an ergometer | Shows progress, confidence, and athletic pressure |
| Stroke and rhythm | The repeated movement cycle performed together | Demonstrates how individual effort becomes team performance |
