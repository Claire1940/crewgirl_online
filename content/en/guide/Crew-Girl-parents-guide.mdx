### Why the Rowing Setting Matters

Rowing is more than a visual backdrop. The sport requires timing, coordination, communication, and trust. Those ideas mirror the characters’ personal conflicts: an individual may have strong ability, but the crew still has to work together.

New viewers do not need prior rowing knowledge to follow the story. The important concepts are easy to connect to the character drama:

- **Crew** refers to the team and the athletes working together.
- **Position** affects how a rower contributes to rhythm, balance, and power.
- **Coxswain** describes the person who directs the boat and communicates with the rowers.
- **Erg** refers to an indoor rowing machine used for training and performance testing.
- **PR erg score** means a personal record achieved on an ergometer.

These terms can help parents explain why a training result or team selection matters to the characters.
