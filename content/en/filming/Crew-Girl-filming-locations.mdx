<Section icon={Compass} title="How to Verify a Crew Girl Location Claim">

Location research often produces confident-looking claims before an official source confirms them. A reliable guide should therefore use a repeatable verification process. Start with Netflix materials, then check production coverage, cast interviews, and local film-office announcements. Social media photographs can provide leads, but they should not be treated as final proof without supporting evidence.

The most useful evidence usually includes a direct statement from Netflix, a production company credit, a recognized local film commission, a cast or crew interview, or a location notice published during filming. A fan post may identify a possible building, but it does not establish that the production filmed there.
